# Microservices Design

## Auth Service

Responsibilities:

- Register
- Login
- JWT
- Refresh Tokens

Database:

- PostgreSQL

Tables:

- users
- refresh_tokens

---

## User Service

Responsibilities:

- Profile Management
- KYC

Tables:

- user_kyc

---

## Account Service

Responsibilities:

- Accounts
- Beneficiaries

Tables:

- accounts
- beneficiaries

---

## Transaction Service

Responsibilities:

- Transfer Money
- Transaction History

Tables:

- transactions

---

## Notification Service

Responsibilities:

- Email
- Notifications

Database:

- MongoDB
